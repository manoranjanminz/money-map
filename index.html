Settings

💾 Backup & restore

Your data is stored only in this browser on this device. Download a backup now and then. It contains all your transactions and every category choice, so you can restore it on another phone or computer.

📤 Export transactions

A spreadsheet of your sorted transactions, handy at tax time or for your own records.

🏷️ Your own categories

Add categories the app doesn’t have. Optional words let it sort matching payments automatically (e.g. “dog, vet, pet” for a Pets category).

📅 When does your month start?

If you plan around payday, start each month on that day (e.g. 25th → 25 Oct – 24 Nov counts as November). Totals, budgets, charts and the score all follow it.

🧭 Your sorting rules

Every time you choose a category for a payee, the app remembers it and sorts that payee's future payments the same way. Remove a rule to go back to automatic sorting.

🔒 App lock

Ask for a PIN when the app opens on this device, and again after 5 minutes away. It stops someone casually opening the app on your phone. It does not encrypt your data, so keep your phone locked too.
